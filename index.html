Fast Delivery Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Fast Delivery ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Fast Delivery mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information provided during account registration.
  • Mobile phone number used for account authentication.
  • Delivery address and location information required to provide delivery services.
  • Order details, including products, quantities, delivery information, and order status.
  • Account and application information needed to provide the requested services.
  • Push notification information, including a device notification token, when notifications are enabled.

2. Location Information

Fast Delivery may request access to your device location when necessary to provide location-based delivery services, such as selecting or confirming a delivery location.

Location permission is requested through the Android operating system. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Provide delivery services.
  • Communicate order and delivery status.
  • Send relevant push notifications.
  • Provide customer and account support.
  • Maintain and improve the application and its services.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the application.

4. Push Notifications

Fast Delivery may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications related to your account, orders, deliveries, and other relevant application activity.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the requested services, operate the application, comply with applicable law, protect the rights and safety of users, or work with service providers that support application functionality.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Your Choices

You may control certain permissions, including location and notifications, through your device settings.

If you want to request information about your personal data or request deletion of your account and associated information, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

Fast Delivery is not intended for children who are unable to legally use the services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services necessary for application functionality, such as Firebase services for push notifications. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact Fast Delivery through the contact information provided within the application.
